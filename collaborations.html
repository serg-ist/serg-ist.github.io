Stellar and Exoplanet Astrophysics Research Group

Collaborations

Astronomy depends on shared telescopes, shared data and shared expertise. We work with researchers at universities and observatories around the world, and we welcome new partnerships.

Our partners

Institutions and networks that support our observations, computing and research.

    Ways to work with us

    • Joint observing campaigns and shared telescope time
    • Photometric data analysis and light-curve modelling
    • Studies of eclipsing binaries, variable stars and exoplanet candidates
    • Co-supervised student projects and research visits
    • Joint publications, workshops and scientific meetings
