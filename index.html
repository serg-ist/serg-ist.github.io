Star and Exoplanet Research Group (SERG)

Reading the light of stars

We study eclipsing binary stars, transiting exoplanets and stellar flares through the way a star’s brightness changes over time: the light curves they leave in our data.

Explore our research Meet the group

What we study

Based at the Institute of Space Technology, Islamabad, our group brings together researchers and students working in observational astrophysics. We use photometry from ground-based telescopes and space missions to measure the properties of stars and the planets around them.

Eclipsing binaries

Pairs of stars whose orbits make them block each other’s light. Their eclipses reveal sizes, temperatures and how close binaries evolve.

More on eclipsing binaries

Transiting exoplanets

Planets that pass in front of their host star, causing a small, regular dip in brightness that tells us the planet’s size and orbit.

More on transiting exoplanets

Stellar flares

Sudden bursts of energy from magnetically active stars, especially cool red dwarfs, with consequences for any planets nearby.

More on stellar flares
