In planning

Summer Research School

A short, hands-on programme introducing students to observational astronomy and the methods used in current research on stars and exoplanets.

Draft programme

The topics below are a draft and may change as the programme is finalised.

  • Observational astronomy

    Telescopes, detectors and how an observing night is planned.

  • Photometry and imaging

    From raw CCD frames to calibrated measurements of brightness.

  • Eclipsing binary light curves

    Reading the shape of an eclipse to learn about two stars.

  • Finding exoplanets

    The transit method, and how to tell a planet from an impostor.

  • Stellar variability and flares

    Why stars change in brightness, from pulsations to flares.

  • Python for astronomy

    Hands-on sessions loading, cleaning and plotting real TESS data.

Who it is for

Undergraduate and early graduate students in physics, space science and related subjects. No previous research experience is needed; some familiarity with Python will help in the hands-on sessions.
