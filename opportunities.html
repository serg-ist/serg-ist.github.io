Star and Exoplanet Research Group (SERG)

Research opportunities

We take on motivated students who want to learn how astronomy research is actually done: working with real data, writing the code to analyse it, and turning the results into a paper.

Who can join

  • BS students looking for a final-year project or research experience
  • MS/MPhil students seeking thesis supervision
  • PhD applicants interested in stellar or exoplanet astrophysics
  • Research interns from IST and other universities, for short-term projects

Possible project areas

  • Eclipsing binaries and other variable stars
  • Exoplanet transit photometry
  • Stellar flares and magnetic activity
  • Astronomical observation and data reduction
  • Scientific programming and research methods

What you will learn

Projects are built around real data, so the skills carry over to graduate study and to data-heavy careers outside astronomy.

  • Reducing CCD images and extracting light curves
  • Finding and working with TESS mission data
  • Modelling eclipsing binaries and fitting transits
  • Python for analysis, statistics and plotting
  • Writing up results in the style of a research paper

How to apply

  1. Choose an area

    Read about our research and pick the topic that interests you most.

  2. Send a short introduction

    Include your CV, your current transcript, a paragraph on why that topic interests you, and any programming experience.

  3. Meet to discuss a project

    If a suitable project is available, Dr. Zubairi will arrange a short meeting to discuss it with you.

Write to us about a project
